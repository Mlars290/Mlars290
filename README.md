### The Challenge

Representing **Metropolitan State University**, my teammate and I competed in the **Association for Information Systems (AIS) Student Chapter Leadership Conference** in the **EY AI-Driven Talent Optimization** competition.

The challenge focused on designing an artificial intelligence solution capable of improving talent acquisition while addressing real-world concerns surrounding fairness, explainability, governance, privacy, and responsible AI.

After advancing through the finalist round, our team earned **1st Place** among the **8 finalist teams** selected from universities across North America.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**Insight Talent AI** is an AI-powered talent intelligence platform designed to help organizations evaluate candidates more transparently and responsibly.

### Key Features

- 🔍 Semantic resume matching
- 🤖 AI-powered candidate recommendations
- 📄 Resume redaction
- ⚖️ Fairness and bias analysis
- 💬 Explainable AI recommendations
- 📊 Audit logs
- 📈 Candidate comparison tools
- 🛡 Responsible AI governance

# Back-to-School 2026: Navigating AI in College

As artificial intelligence becomes increasingly common in higher education, I noticed many students were using AI tools but weren't always sure how to use them effectively or ethically.

Rather than focusing only on the capabilities of AI, the article explores how students can develop healthy habits for using these tools throughout their education and future careers.
