## 🌷 hi, i'm Maria

I'm studying **Data Science at Metropolitan State University**, while exploring how data, AI, design, and storytelling can work together.

I enjoy shaping ideas into things people can understand and use: a machine learning project, an intuitive interface, a visual identity, or a story that makes technology feel approachable. My interests span **applied AI, UX/UI, creative strategy, and visual content design**.

Outside my data projects, you'll find me designing pastel visuals, experimenting with AI creative tools, and developing the characters and world of **Lil’ Jammies**. ♡

- **Creative Director & Copy Editor · The Metropolitan** — developing visual direction for the newspaper and The Metroscope, creating promotional designs and brand assets, and editing and writing technology stories.
- **AI Studio Fellow · Break Through Tech** — collaborating on an Abt Global project exploring LLM-assisted migration of legacy SAS workflows to Python, with validation and documentation.
- **Lil’ Jammies · Personal creative project** — building an original character world through illustration, storytelling, and experiments with AI-assisted visuals and animation.
